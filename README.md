# 🌍 Climate Crusade – SDG 13

**Climate Crusade** is a browser-based game designed to raise awareness about climate change through interactive, level-based gameplay.

Players take on the role of **Alvin**, an extraterrestrial hero sent to restore Earth’s health by neutralizing environmental threats across different regions.

Alvin’s species travels across the galaxy saving planets from destruction. They use high-tech devices to assess planetary health and have the ability to transform into different objects or life forms. To avoid suspicion on Earth, Alvin transforms into humans or objects depending on the environment. This is why players control different characters or objects in each level.

**A more detailed description of the project is included in the `project proposal` document in this repository.**

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

- Create an account or log in  
- First-time players experience an introductory cutscene  
- Select levels from a central hub  
- Collect stars and complete environmental objectives  
- Avoid hazards and solve engaging challenges  
- Receive educational tips at the end of each level  

---

## 🌦 Current Levels

- **Level 01 – Acid Downpour**  
  A platformer focused on acid rain and emission systems.

- **Level 02 – Deep Purge**  
  An underwater cleanup mission addressing water pollution.

- **Level 03 – Sunken Wells**  
  A desert-based challenge centered around water scarcity, sandstorms, and land erosion.

- **Level 04 – Space Junk**  
  A space-level mission targeting space debris.

## 🚀 Running the Project

1. Since the project uses a free-tier backend, you must first wake the server by visiting:  
   https://climate-crusade.onrender.com/

2. The server may take up to 50 seconds to start. It will display **"Backend is running"** once ready. After 10 minutes of inactivity, it will shut down automatically, so repeat Step 1 if needed.

3. Access the game at:  
   https://saagee-studios.github.io/climate-crusade/

4. Sign up and log in using valid credentials.

5. Enjoy the experience.

## 🔎 Extra Instructions

This section includes gameplay details that were not explicitly stated in-game due to time and space constraints. These will be incorporated directly into future updates.

1. When starting a level (after clicking **Play**), a title card will appear.  
   Pressing the **Esc** key on the title card will return you to the Level Select screen.

2. While playing a level, pressing **Esc** will pause the game.  
   You will be given the option to **Exit** or **Continue**.  

   - Choosing **Exit** returns you to the level’s title card.  
   - From the title card, press **Esc** again to return to the Level Select screen.
